About

My dissertation research, advised by Professor Sudipto Banerjee, is in Bayesian spatial data analysis, particularly methods for detecting and measuring spatial health disparities. It has led to two published papers so far, with a third under review. Outside my dissertation, I'm collaborating with Professor W. K. Wong of UCLA and Dr. Pavlos Msaouel of UT MD Anderson on basket trial designs, and at Genentech I work on external control information borrowing methods and dynamic trial monitoring.

Before Genentech, I worked as a statistcal graduate researcher at Boeing Research and Technology, and earlier built a dashboard of pre-screening recruitment trends for an Alzheimer's disease trial at the USC Alzheimer's Therapeutic Research Institute.

Research

  • Detecting spatial health disparities

    With Professor Banerjee, I developed a Bayesian hierarchical approach that finds health disparities across geographic regions, and studied how spatial confounding distorts multivariate areal models. A third manuscript, on detecting disease boundaries, is under review. It uses neural posterior estimation, a machine learning approach that trains a neural network to approximate the posterior so that inference on new data is fast.

    Published in Biostatistics and Biometrics. See publications.

  • Borrowing external controls in trial monitoring

    At Genentech I'm studying how Bayesian dynamic borrowing affects predictive probability of success as an interim monitoring statistic, including scenarios where the external data conflict with the current trial.

  • Efficient basket trial designs

    With Professor W. K. Wong of UCLA and Dr. Pavlos Msaouel of UT MD Anderson, I'm working on optimal two-stage designs for basket trials that use Bayesian local exchangeability models to conduct dynamic information borrowing.

    Manuscript under review.

  • Intimate partner violence and covert contraception use

    I led the statistical analysis of how covert contraceptive use relates to forms of domestic violence in the Rakai Community Cohort Study in Uganda. I also analyzed pilot data from domestic violence lethality screens collected by LAPD first responders.

    Published in Reproductive Health. See publications.
